# FT remote-primitive choice: GO/NO-GO report

**Decision: NO_GO**

Generated by `experiments/ftprim/report.py` from `SUMMARY.json`, `FITS.json` and `MECHANISM.json`. Criteria and regime classes are those frozen in `EXPERIMENT_CONTRACT.md`.

## Decision rationale

* NO_GO triggers fired: small improvement; GO criteria failed: G1, G2, G3, G7.
* On 160 communicating x realistic instances the failure-optimal oracle beats the ebit-optimal baseline by a median factor between 1 and 1.01 per regime (max 1.35); 3% of instances reach 1.25x and none reach 10x.
* The solver-independent bound on the true benefit is at most 2.54 in realistic regimes (median 1.11) and at most 3.7 even with ideal links or free entanglement, so the order-of-magnitude criterion cannot be met by a better solver.
* Decisions do differ: 15% of realistic and 71% of idealised instances differ on >= 10% of decisions, almost always in one direction (the ebit objective teleports where the failure objective keeps the non-local CNOT; every clean-pattern inversion is of this kind), but the consequence is small.
* G5 passes only vacuously: in realistic regimes no burst threshold beats the ebit-optimal plan (negative recovery), because that plan is already within a few percent of the failure oracle.
* Where the failure oracle wins in idealised regimes, it spends a median 3.49x (max 8.24x) the Bell pairs of the ebit-optimal plan, and a burst threshold chosen per regime recovers 90% of its benefit.

## 6. Limitations

* Logical failure is the TMCBS single-basis (Z-readout, X-error) convention of the paper; the composition into programs is additive and validated only on SC d=3 hold-outs (section 4).
* SC d=7 costs are statistically unresolved (every d=7 point hit its shot cap with 5-76 failures); d=7 enters the workload matrix with wide intervals and clamped non-negative costs.
* Magic-state (T) failures, single-qubit gates, decoder latency and classical feed-forward latency are not modelled; they are identical across policies and would only dilute the benefit further.
* Two QPUs, a Kernighan-Lin static partition and lock-step layers; the four largest sources (factor247_n15, vqe_n24, hhl_n14, hhl_n10) were excluded for oracle tractability.
* The oracle is CP-SAT with time limits; where it lacks an optimality certificate the solver-independent bound (section 2) caps what a better solver could find.
* Only transversal non-local CNOT and TMCBS logical teleportation were compared; lattice-surgery interfaces, cat-state (multi-gate) sharing and entanglement distillation were not simulated.
